What you'll do

Leave Kaş at 09:30. We board Captain Ergün's wooden gulet at the neighbouring port (transfer included). The route follows the Lycian coast over the sunken ruins of Simena — visible through the clear water — past Kekova island, with stops in the hidden coves for swimming. Slow lunch on board. Free hour in the castle village of Kaleköy. Back to Kaş by sunset.
